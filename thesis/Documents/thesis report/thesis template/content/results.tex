\subsubsection{Ablation: Age Binning Granularity}
\label{sec:results:rq2:utility:age-binning}

The preprocessing pipeline (\S\ref{sec:data:fe:age}) retains the dataset's
native ten-decade encoding of \texttt{age} after an earlier design that
collapsed it into three coarse groups (young / middle-aged / elderly) was
abandoned. Table~\ref{tab:age-binning-tstr} quantifies the impact on
Train-on-Synthetic, Test-on-Real (TSTR) utility for all three non-DP
generators (PATE-GAN, HealthGAN, Pythia-70M), evaluated on the real
held-out test split ($n = 14{,}304$) with \texttt{readmitted} as the
binary target. Gradient Boosting is reported as the headline downstream
classifier; results for Logistic Regression and Random Forest follow the
same qualitative pattern.
\vspace{0.5em}

Two observations frame the analysis. First, the Train-on-Real upper bound
is essentially invariant between the two encodings (TRTR AUC-ROC: $0.7008$
under ten-bin vs.\ $0.7014$ under three-bin), confirming that any
generator-side difference cannot be attributed to a shift in the real-data
signal. Second, the three generators react to the binning choice in
qualitatively different ways, exposing the encoding sensitivity of each
synthesis family.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\caption{TSTR utility under the discarded three-bin age encoding versus
the retained ten-bin encoding. Test set: real held-out split
($n = 14{,}304$); target: \texttt{readmitted}; classifier: Gradient
Boosting. $\Delta$ reports the change from three-bin to ten-bin.}
\label{tab:age-binning-tstr}
\begin{tabular}{llccc}
\toprule
Train Source & Metric & 3-bin & 10-bin & $\Delta$ (10 $-$ 3) \\
\midrule
Real (TRTR) & AUC-ROC & 0.7014 & 0.7008 & $-0.0006$ \\
Real (TRTR) & F1      & 0.3644 & 0.3668 & $+0.0024$ \\
\midrule
PATE-GAN    & AUC-ROC & 0.4823 & 0.5108 & $+0.0285$ \\
PATE-GAN    & F1      & 0.0437 & 0.0093 & $-0.0344$ \\
HealthGAN   & AUC-ROC & 0.6605 & 0.6312 & $-0.0293$ \\
HealthGAN   & F1      & 0.2647 & 0.3824 & $+0.1177$ \\
Pythia-70M  & AUC-ROC & 0.5981 & \textbf{0.6634} & $\mathbf{+0.0653}$ \\
Pythia-70M  & F1      & 0.3944 & \textbf{0.5005} & $\mathbf{+0.1061}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\paragraph{Pythia-70M: large gain under finer binning.}
The token-based LLM generator benefits unambiguously from the ten-decade
encoding: AUC-ROC rises by $+0.0653$ ($0.5981 \rightarrow 0.6634$) and
macro-F1 by $+0.1061$ ($0.3944 \rightarrow 0.5005$), with the same
direction observed under Logistic Regression
($\Delta\text{AUC} = +0.0442$, $\Delta\text{F1} = +0.0992$) and Random
Forest ($\Delta\text{AUC} = +0.0671$, $\Delta\text{F1} = +0.1123$).
Because each field is emitted as a discrete token, the three-bin scheme
collapsed the entire $0$--$100$ year range into three vocabulary items,
erasing conditional structure between age and clinically correlated
features (\texttt{number\_diagnoses}, \texttt{number\_inpatient},
\texttt{insulin}). The ten-decade encoding restores a finer conditioning
vocabulary and recovers age-specific co-occurrence patterns relevant to
readmission prediction.

\paragraph{HealthGAN: metric-dependent direction.}
HealthGAN exhibits the opposite ranking behaviour to Pythia: AUC-ROC is
slightly \emph{higher} under three-bin ($0.6605$ vs.\ $0.6312$,
$\Delta = -0.0293$ moving to ten-bin). The threshold-based F1, however,
moves in the reverse direction, $0.2647 \rightarrow 0.3824$
($+0.1177$ in favour of ten-bin), driven primarily by recall recovery
($0.1697 \rightarrow 0.2984$). The interpretation is that HealthGAN did
not capture the fine-grained ten-bin age structure: under the coarser
encoding its distributional output is closer to the simpler target and
yields marginally better probability ranking, but the synthetic data
over-concentrates on the majority (non-readmit) class and suppresses
positive-class recall. The ten-bin encoding therefore degrades AUC
marginally while restoring usable clinical decision performance.

\paragraph{PATE-GAN: differential-privacy noise dominates.}
PATE-GAN remains close to chance under both encodings (AUC-ROC $0.4823$
three-bin vs.\ $0.5108$ ten-bin); the binning choice produces only a
$+0.0285$ AUC shift, an order of magnitude smaller than the Pythia
delta. F1 is near zero in either case ($0.0437$ vs.\ $0.0093$). The
DP-PATE noise mechanism dominates over the encoding-level signal, so the
generator does not meaningfully exploit either age representation. This
sets the floor against which the within-family DP-utility cost is later
quantified in \S\ref{sec:results:rq2:dp-cost}.
\vspace{0.5em}

Taken together, the three generators react to the same preprocessing
change in three distinct regimes: a token-based LLM that gains
substantially from finer categorical cardinality, a continuous-latent GAN
whose ranking and threshold behaviour diverge under the change, and a
DP-noised GAN that is largely insensitive to it. This generator-specific
encoding sensitivity is discussed further in
Section~\ref{sec:discussion:gan-vs-llm}.
